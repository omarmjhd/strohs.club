Welcome. Whether you found us through No Laying Up, a friend, or a lucky Discord invite, here's the short version of who we are and how everything fits together.

## What Is a Roost?

A Roost is a group of golfers — usually organized by geography — that grew out of the No Laying Up (NLU) message-board community. NLU's board is known as "The Nest," and as members started organizing golf events on their own, "Roost" became a natural, tongue-in-cheek riff on the Nest theme.

## Who We Are

We're the **South Texas Roost of Hospitality** — STROH's — with members spread across Austin, Houston, and San Antonio.

The name is half a joke:

- It's a friendly jab at Dallas.
- The old Stroh's beer logo looks fantastic once you tweak the lion to hold a golf club.

Honestly? It's very possible the whole "Roost" idea got reverse-engineered just so STROH's would work as an acronym.

## Why We Exist

At its heart, the Roost exists to field a **4-golfer team** chasing a spot in the **Roost Club Championship**, hosted by NLU at Sweetens Cove Golf Club in Tennessee each late October.

We compete in the **Texahoma Region** alongside Big Plex (DFW) and Supercell (Oklahoma). We square off against them at **Roost Regionals** in late July / early August, where the championship berth is on the line.

Being part of STROH's means committing to the qualifiers to the best of your ability — and, if you earn it, the national tournament.

## The NIT

Separately, NLU hosts the **Nest Invitational Tournament (NIT)** every November. Each Roost names one of its events its "Major," and that event's winner becomes the Roost's NIT qualifier.

Our Major is the **Texas Two-Step**.

## How You Get on the Team

Four qualifiers feed our Roost Regional team:

- **Texas Two-Step** — our Major
- **All-STROHs Open**
- **OpnSzn Match Play**
- **[Muni Tour](/competitions/muni-tour)**

Dedicated pages for the others are coming soon.

## Our Governing Principles

1. **Keep the Main Thing the Main Thing** The hang, meeting new friends, and the charity matter every bit as much as the golf.
2. **All Are Welcome** Come out and play — and bring friends.
3. **Don't Be a Jerk** Assume good intent.
4. **Competition Matters (This Is 4th on Purpose)** We know the rules, keep score, and win and lose with grace.

Our motto says it best: **Count shots, hit cups.**

## The Executive Committee

If a qualifier's winner can't make it to Regionals or Nationals, the runner-up is first in line as alternate. Beyond that, the Executive Committee hand-picks alternates so we can always field a team without a scramble — prioritizing the players who've competed best over the year, while respecting that everyone has a day job.

## Charity

Each season the Roost runs a charity donation drive, and members get creative with how they give — think "$1 for every net birdie." For 2026, the charity returns to **Wonders and Worries**; past seasons have also supported the Austin Firefighters Relief & Outreach Fund.

## Getting Involved

The best first step is to [hop in our Discord](https://discord.gg/frNSUn5ZmC) — that's where everything happens. **@jdonelson** and **@omarmjhdpr** are happy to help, too.

It can feel like a lot at first, so here's the cheat code: head straight to the **#find-a-game** channel, introduce yourself, grab an open spot, and come play golf. That's really all it takes.
