## What We're About

- **Keep the Main Thing the Main Thing** — The hang, new friends, and charity matter as much as the golf.
- **All Are Welcome** — Come out and play. Seriously. Bring your friends, too.
- **Don't Be a Jerk** — Assume good intent in all interactions.
- **Competition Matters (This Is 4th on Purpose)** — We know the rules. We win and lose with grace.

## New Here? Start in Three Steps

New here? [▶ Watch the 5-minute intro slideshow.](/downloads/strohs-overview-slides.html)

1. **Join the Discord** — [hop in our Discord](https://discord.gg/frNSUn5ZmC). It can look busy at first, but head straight to `#find-a-game`.
2. **Post that you're looking to play** — introduce yourself and grab an open spot.
3. **Come play golf.** That's it. The competitions will make sense once you're out there.
