Wrapped around the competitions is the part that actually keeps the Roost going: a community of
golfers from all walks of life, and a place to find people to play with, talk about architecture
and life, and stay connected.

## Discord

We maintain an active Discord community, and it's where everything happens — scheduling, scoring,
arguing about tee boxes, all of it. [Hop in our Discord](https://discord.gg/frNSUn5ZmC), or ask
**@jdonelson**, **@omarmjhdpr**, or any other STROH on the Refuge for an invite link.

The Discord can be overwhelming at first, but it gets easier — promise.

## Start in #find-a-game

Here's the cheat code. Go straight to the **#find-a-game** channel.

It's a rare weekend where someone doesn't have an open spot or two somewhere nearby, and anyone
is free to jump in. Post in that channel, ask if the spot is still available, introduce
yourself, and come play golf. That's really all it takes.

## This website

We keep a group website at [strohs.club](https://www.strohs.club). It's primarily here to keep
track of the results from our events, but it also carries a copy of the manifesto and, now and
then, writeups of things we've done. It's worth a bookmark.
