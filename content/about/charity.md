Every season, the Roost sponsors **at least one charity** for a season-long donation drive. It
sits alongside the golf rather than behind it — raising money for a good cause is written into
[our first governing principle](/about/governing-principles), not bolted on at the end.

## How members give

Roost members are encouraged to find creative ways to maximize what we hand over at the end of
the year. The classic version is a season-long birdie or eagle challenge you set for yourself:

> "I'll donate $1 for every net birdie I make."

Tie it to something you'd be doing anyway — muni rounds, match-play wins, a Two-Step ringer card
— and the total takes care of itself. We're always open to creative ideas, and we'd rather have
ten people running small, silly challenges than one large cheque.

## This season

Our charity is **Wonders and Worries**, which provides free professional support to children
and teenagers during a parent's serious illness.

## Who we've supported

Past seasons have gone to:

- The **Austin Firefighters Relief and Outreach Fund**
- **Wonders and Worries**

Our charity doesn't change every year — carrying the same cause across seasons is normal and
deliberate — but we're open to new suggestions at any time. If there's an organization that
matters to you, bring it to the Discord.
