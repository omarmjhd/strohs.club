Winning a qualifier earns you a seat on the Roost Regional team. Life, unfortunately, does not
check the golf calendar first — so the Executive Committee exists to fill the seats that come
open without turning it into a scramble.

## Alternates for Regionals and Nationals

If the winner of a qualifying event can't attend the Roost Regional or the Roost Club
Championship, the **runner-up from that event is the first alternate**. Beyond that, the
Executive Committee reserves the right to hand-pick alternates so we can always field a team
without undue thrash.

The Committee makes every effort to involve high finishers across the four qualifying events in
that decision. There is usually enough time to ask the runners-up from *other* events to fill in
when both the winner and runner-up from a given event are out.

We used to run a fixed alternates list instead, and it didn't work. Being bound by the list
structure meant it took entirely too long to fill out a team — the next name down was often the
one person who couldn't go, and there was no way to skip past them. The current approach trades
that rigidity for judgement.

The goal behind that judgement is simple: the players who have played the best over the course
of the year should get their chance to represent STROH's at the national events — balanced
against the fact that everyone has a day job.

## Naming the NIT qualifier

The NIT alternate situation is out of our hands, and the deadline is tight.

- Once our Major finishes — currently the [Texas Two-Step](/competitions/two-step) — we have
  **seven days** to name our NIT qualifier.
- We **do not have to name the winner**. If the champion already knows they can't make a
  November tournament, we can name someone else.
- Once we name a qualifier, **we cannot change it.** The pick is irrevocable, so the Committee
  makes it deliberately.

To give everyone a fair shot at planning, the Committee works to communicate the NIT dates as
early as possible in the competition year.
