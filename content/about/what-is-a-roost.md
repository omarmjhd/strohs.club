A Roost is a group of golfers — usually organized by geography — that grew out of the No Laying
Up (NLU) message-board community. NLU's board is known as "The Nest," and as members started
organizing golf events on their own, "Roost" became a natural, tongue-in-cheek riff on the Nest
theme.

## Who We Are

We're the **South Texas Roost of Hospitality** — STROH's — with members spread across Austin,
Houston, and San Antonio.

The name is half a joke:

- It's a friendly jab at Dallas.
- The old Stroh's beer logo looks fantastic once you tweak the lion to hold a golf club.

Honestly? It's very possible the whole "Roost" idea got reverse-engineered just so STROH's would
work as an acronym.

As for *why* we exist: the Roost fields a four-golfer team chasing a spot in the Roost Club
Championship. [The Path to Nationals](/season/path-to-nationals) walks through how that works.
